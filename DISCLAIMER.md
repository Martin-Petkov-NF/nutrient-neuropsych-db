# Disclaimer

This repository is a bibliography. It is a record of what published research
says, with each entry traceable to its source.

**It is not medical advice and it is not clinical guidance.** It does not tell
anyone what to take, what to test for, or what to do about a result. Decisions
about medication, supplementation and testing belong with a qualified clinician
who knows the individual.

**Entries are not endorsements.** The `direction` field records whether a paper
supports a claim, fails to support it, or found nothing. Papers that find
nothing are collected deliberately. A citation appearing here means the paper
exists and has been checked for what it says, not that its conclusion is correct.

**Evidence grades are judgements.** They are set by reviewers, they are
contestable, and the place to contest one is an issue or a pull request.
