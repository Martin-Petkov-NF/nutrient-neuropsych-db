**What the columns mean.** "What it says" is the contributor's one-sentence summary.
"Finding" is whether the paper supports the claim, fails to support it, or found nothing.
"Strength" is the reviewer's grade of how good the evidence is.
"Checked" says whether a person has read the paper yet.

**An entry marked "not yet read" passed the automatic checks and nothing more.** The paper
exists, it has not been retracted, it is not a duplicate, and its details came from the
PubMed record rather than being retyped. Nobody has confirmed that the summary describes
what the paper actually found. Treat those entries with more caution than verified ones.
